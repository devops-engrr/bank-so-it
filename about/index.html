About Bank SO IT

Focused educational content for Bank Specialist Officer and banking-exam preparation.

Our purpose

Bank SO IT brings together concept explanations, practice questions, quizzes, revision resources and exam-focused strategies to make preparation more structured and consistent.

Subjects

Professional Knowledge topics include DBMS, Networking, Operating Systems, Data Structures, OOP, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Cloud and more. We also publish English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning and Banking Awareness content relevant to banking examinations.

Exams

Content may cover IBPS SO IT Officer, SBI SO, RRB SO and other IT-based Bank SO examinations, as well as common banking-exam subjects.

LEARN FROM EXPERIENCE

Practical engineering behind the learning

Bank SO IT is created and guided by a Senior Cloud & DevOps Engineer with hands-on experience building, deploying, troubleshooting and operating cloud-native platforms and production infrastructure.

The teaching approach focuses on understanding concepts from an engineering perspective rather than memorising isolated facts. Where useful, technical topics are connected to practical systems, production scenarios and the way technologies work in real environments.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Cloud infrastructure, AWS, GCP and Azure concepts, virtual machines, networking and production deployment.

DevOps & CI/CD

Docker, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, TeamCity, automation, Linux and infrastructure workflows.

Kubernetes & Containers

Kubernetes, K3s, EKS, AKS, Helm, container platforms, ingress, gateways and cloud-native deployment patterns.

Observability & Security

OpenTelemetry, monitoring, distributed systems, logging, tracing, security practices and production troubleshooting.

Why this matters for Bank SO IT preparation

The goal is not simply to help you remember an answer, but to help you understand why the answer is correct and how the underlying concept works in practice.

Whether you are preparing for IBPS SO IT, RRB SO IT, SBI SO or another IT-focused banking examination, the platform follows a practical learning cycle:

Concept → Practical Understanding → MCQs → PYQs → Revision → Tests
