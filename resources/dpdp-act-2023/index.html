01 · CORE DEFINITIONS

Know the roles

Data Principal
The individual to whom the personal data relates. The Act's definition also addresses a child and a person with disability through the relevant parent or lawful guardian.
Data Fiduciary
The person who alone or together with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. A bank making these decisions may act as a Data Fiduciary.
Data Processor
A person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary. The actual arrangement and activity matter more than the vendor's label.
Consent Manager
A person registered with the Board that acts as a single point of contact for a Data Principal to give, manage, review and withdraw consent through an accessible, transparent and interoperable platform.
Child
An individual who has not completed 18 years of age, as defined by the Act.
02 · SCOPE & CONSENT

Scope, lawful processing and consent

  • The Act applies to digital personal data processed within India when collected digitally or collected non-digitally and digitised subsequently, subject to the Act.
  • It can also apply to processing outside India when connected with offering goods or services to Data Principals within India.
  • Processing must be for a lawful purpose and based on consent or a recognised “certain legitimate use”, as applicable.
  • Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, indicated through clear affirmative action, and limited to personal data necessary for the specified purpose.
  • Consent can be withdrawn. The withdrawal process should be comparable in ease to the process of giving consent; withdrawal does not automatically erase separate legal retention duties.
  • When considering an erasure request, distinguish the individual's rights from applicable legal or regulatory retention requirements.
03 · RIGHTS & DUTIES

Rights and organisational responsibilities

  • Data Principal rights include access to information about personal data, correction and completion, erasure subject to applicable provisions, grievance redressal, and nomination.
  • Data Fiduciaries have duties under the Act, including taking reasonable security safeguards and notifying personal data breaches as required by the applicable provisions.
  • Do not assume every bank activity, every financial-data operation, or every regulated organisation is automatically exempt. Exemptions depend on the provision and its conditions.
  • A Data Processor processes on behalf of a Data Fiduciary; classify the relationship based on the actual processing arrangement.
04 · BOARD & PENALTIES

Data Protection Board and penalty framework

  • The Data Protection Board of India is established under Section 18. Section 27 addresses the Board's powers and functions.
  • The inquiry process includes an opportunity of being heard in accordance with the Act.
  • The Schedule sets maximum penalty amounts for specified contraventions. A maximum is not an automatic fixed penalty in every case.
  • The Schedule includes maxima of ₹250 crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards, ₹200 crore for failure to notify the Board and affected Data Principals of a breach, ₹150 crore for specified Significant Data Fiduciary obligations, and ₹50 crore for a contravention for which no separate penalty is specified.
  • Section 33(2) lists factors used when determining a monetary penalty, including the nature, gravity and duration of the breach; type and nature of data affected; repetition; gain or loss avoided; mitigation; proportionality and deterrence; and the likely impact of the penalty on the person.
  • Section 34 says penalty sums realised by the Board are credited to the Consolidated Fund of India.

06 · COMMENCEMENT TIMELINE

Do not mix up notification and commencement

11 August 2023

The DPDP Act, 2023 received Presidential assent.

13 November 2025

The Act's commencement notification specified different phases: some provisions immediately, Section 6(9) and Section 27(1)(d) one year later, and another group eighteen months later.

14 November 2025

The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified. Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 commenced on publication; Rule 4 is scheduled one year later; Rules 3, 5–16 and 22–23 are scheduled eighteen months later.

Exam trap

The Act and Rules have different notification dates. Calculate their phased commencement dates separately and confirm the latest official notifications.
